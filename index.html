Community Framework

Point 05 Governance Framework

Proposed community decision-making for project initiatives. Initial polling is planned via Telegram, with a proposed eligibility threshold of >1,000 ECAT for future formal voting. Dedicated blockchain voting platforms may be developed later.

Point 06 Proposed Community Fund

A future fund envisioned to support cultural, technological, and creative initiatives through voluntary contributions and treasury allocations. Formal legal and custody structures must be established before any funds are collected or deployed.

Point 07 Greek Island Space

The first proposed community decision involves developing a physical gathering space on a Greek island for "Social Choreography" and creative collaboration, targeted for Summer 2027. This requires separate funding, due diligence, and a legal community organization.

Point 08 Wider Ecosystem

ECAT operates as an independent meme-token movement. It is distinct from the ANARK token concept and the proposed Poshanarchist political organization. ECAT ownership does not confer political membership or voting rights in outside entities.

Executive overview

Designed for token discovery and investor-grade storytelling across every viewport.

ECAT (European Cat) is an ESDT meme token on MultiversX. The European Cat symbolises curiosity, humour and the ability to make independent choices. Its proposed community programme includes creative participation, transparent project decisions and, in the longer term, cultural and educational activities beyond the blockchain. The first proposed physical project is a gathering space on a Greek island. ECAT also explores the Social Choreography concept: cultural and creative experiences through which people exchange perspectives, collaborate and develop new ideas. Neither land acquisition nor a community fund has yet been established.

The $1 million market-capitalisation figure is a proposed project milestone. Not a treasury balance, not fundraising result. Not token-price forecast nor guarantee of available funds.
